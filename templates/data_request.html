Exercise Your GDPR Rights

Use this form to request access to your data, request deletion, or exercise other GDPR rights.

Important Information

Response Time: We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR.

Identity Verification: We may contact you to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

Free of Charge: The first request is free. Excessive or repetitive requests may incur a reasonable fee.

Automatic Deletion: All data is automatically deleted after 90 days, so you may not need to request deletion.

Common Requests Explained

Access Request

We will provide you with all questions and answers associated with your IP address, including timestamps and metadata.

Deletion Request

We will delete all data associated with your IP address from our systems. This action is irreversible.

Objection

If you object to our data processing, we will stop processing your data. However, this means you won't be able to use the service (rate limiting requires logging).

Complaints

If you're not satisfied with how we handle your data or your request, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:

Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens

Website: autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl
