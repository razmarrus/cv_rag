When you use this CV question-answering system, we collect:

  • IP Address: Your device's internet protocol address
  • Questions: The questions you submit to the system
  • Answers: The responses generated by our AI system
  • Metadata: Timestamp, response time, number of document chunks used

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Rate Limiting: To prevent abuse and ensure fair access to the service (10 questions per day per IP)
  • Service Improvement: To understand usage patterns and improve system quality
  • Debugging: To identify and fix technical issues
  • Security: To detect and prevent malicious activity

We process your data based on legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Our legitimate interest: Protecting the service from abuse and ensuring availability for all users.

Balancing test: We collect minimal data (only what's necessary for rate limiting), use short retention periods (90 days), and provide transparent information about our processing.

You have the right to object to this processing. However, objecting may prevent you from using the service as rate limiting is essential for fair access.

Retention period: 90 days from collection

Automatic deletion: Data older than 90 days is automatically deleted from our systems

Why this period: Sufficient for rate limiting (daily reset) and service improvement while minimizing data storage

HuggingFace: Your questions and retrieved context are sent to HuggingFace's API for large language model inference to generate answers.

HuggingFace processes this data according to their privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy

Data location: Our database is hosted in the EU (Netherlands). HuggingFace processing location may vary - see their policy.

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access (Article 15): You can request a copy of all data we hold about you, including all questions and answers associated with your IP address.

Right to Rectification (Article 16): If any data we hold about you is inaccurate, you can request correction.

Right to Erasure (Article 17): You can request deletion of all your data from our systems. This will be processed within 30 days.

Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): You can request that we limit how we use your data.

Right to Object (Article 21): You can object to our processing of your data. Note that this may prevent you from using the service.

Right to Data Portability (Article 20): You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).

Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl)
